Slicing game · 12 weeks · about 5 minutes

Slice it right

Write how you’d slice this feature, put the slices in order, and see how your plan plays out over twelve weeks: for customers, and for the business.

The brief

“As a user, I want to be able to cancel an order by myself”

On this ecommerce platform, cancelling an order means calling the call centre. The business wants self-service cancellation on the website and the iOS and Android apps. You have 12 weeks. How would you slice it to deliver value early, and learn or reduce risk?

Your slices

How would you slice it?

One sticky per slice, in the order you’d build them. Say what changes and where, like “Customers can request a cancellation on the website”. Drag the ⋮⋮ handle to reorder (press and hold on a phone).

Twelve weeks later

How your plan played out

Two lines: customers, the share of cancellations they can do online instead of phoning, and business, the share of call-centre work that goes away. Both only step up when a slice goes live.

Prototype: the estimates come from a simple keyword model for now, not AI.
