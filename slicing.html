Slicing game · 12 weeks · about 5 minutes

Slice it right

Same work, same team, same twelve weeks. The only thing you choose is how to slice it. Watch when the value actually arrives.

The brief

“As a user, I want to be able to cancel an order by myself”

On this ecommerce platform, cancelling an order means calling the call centre. The business wants self-service cancellation on the website and the iOS and Android apps. You have 12 weeks, and there's 22 weeks of work on the table. You won't build it all.

  1. 1. Make slice cardsCreate a card for each slice and give it a name, like “Book by email”.
  2. 2. Drag work inDrag pieces of work onto your cards, then drag the cards into order. On a phone, press and hold to drag.
  3. 3. Value lands at the endNothing counts until a slice ships. Customers only benefit when a capability is live on the channel they use. Across all customers: see if an order can be cancelled +1/wk, request a cancellation +5/wk, cancel it yourself +10/wk.
  4. 4. Estimates lieYou assume customers are split evenly across web, iOS and Android, and that edge cases and polish are worth it. You find out what's true when things ship, or sooner if you test first.
