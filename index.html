Scientist turned agilist

Hello, I'm David

Before I worked with product teams, I was a scientist. I did a PhD in physical chemistry and spent the early part of my career in research, then moved into software development and later worked as a business analyst and project manager.

That background stayed with me. I approach organisations the way I approached research: understand the system, question assumptions, look for evidence, run experiments and learn from what happens.

For more than a decade I've helped teams and organisations improve how they deliver products and services. My focus is value delivery: how organisations identify value, organise work around it, reduce uncertainty, and find out quickly whether what they're doing is making a difference.
