Slicing game · 12 weeks · about 5 minutes

Slice it right

Same work, same team, same twelve weeks. The only thing you choose is how to slice it. Watch when the value actually arrives.

The brief

Let people book a place on a training course

A small training company takes bookings by phone. They want customers to book online. You have 12 weeks, and there's 20 weeks of work on the table. You won't build it all.

  1. 1. Pick piecesTap the pieces you want in your first slice. Each one shows its effort in weeks.
  2. 2. Make slicesGroup them into a slice, then do it again. Slices are built one after the other.
  3. 3. Value lands at the endNothing counts until a slice ships. A customer needs a way to find, book and get confirmed before anything is worth anything. +5/wk
  4. 4. Estimates lieGetting paid adds +3/wk. Nice-to-haves say +1/wk, but that's a guess. You find out the real value when they ship.
