To install in Gmail (on a computer): click Copy signature, then in Gmail go to Settings (cog) → See all settings → General → Signature → Create new (or pick the account it's for), click into the box and press Ctrl+V. Scroll down and Save changes.
If the button doesn't work, drag-select just the signature above and press Ctrl+C.
The Gmail phone app can't show image signatures in its own settings, but emails sent from the phone still use the web signature if "Mobile signature" is left off.
